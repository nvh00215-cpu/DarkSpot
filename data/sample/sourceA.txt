# Global Horizon STEM Excellence Scholarship (Official Portal)
**Annual Undergraduate & Graduate Awards Program**

### Program Overview
The Global Horizon STEM Excellence Scholarship supports outstanding full-time students enrolled in accredited science, technology, engineering, or mathematics programs. The program awards a merit stipend of $12,500 per academic year.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Applicants must be currently enrolled full-time in an accredited undergraduate STEM program (junior or senior standing) or first-year graduate program.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.65 on a 4.0 scale.
- Demonstrated financial need or citizenship status is not restricted; international students are welcome to apply.

### Application Deadline
The final application deadline for the 2026-2027 academic session is strictly November 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern Standard Time. Incomplete or late submissions will not be reviewed under any circumstances.

### Required Application Documents
All applicants must submit the following items through our official applicant portal:
1. Official university academic transcript showing coursework through Spring 2026.
2. Two letters of academic recommendation from departmental professors.
3. A 750-word personal statement detailing your STEM research background and career objectives.
4. Proof of household income via the previous tax year's Official W-2 Form or equivalent national tax return.

### Application Process
Create an account on portal.globalhorizonstem.org, complete the personal profile section, upload all PDF documents, and submit before the deadline. There are no application fees.
