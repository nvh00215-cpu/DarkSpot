# STEM Grant Watch & Student Financial Tips
**Published on: October 15, 2026 | By Sarah Jenkins, Senior Academic Advisor**

### How to Land the Global Horizon STEM Scholarship this Fall
The Global Horizon STEM Excellence Award continues to be one of the highest-value awards open to tech students this season, offering up to $12,500. Having coached several applicants through the submission system, here are the critical guidelines to watch out for.

### Eligibility and Selection
The foundation looks closely at research aptitude and GPA. While they officially state a 3.65 minimum GPA, competitive candidates usually possess 3.8+ along with independent lab experience.

### Crucial Note on Required Documents (Conflict Alert)
Pay special attention to the financial verification requirements:
While older materials asked for W-2 forms, the updated compliance guidelines require submission of an Signed IRS Form 1040 (pages 1 and 2), and specifically state that standalone W-2 slips are NOT accepted as proof of income. If you upload only a W-2, your application may be disqualified during initial document screening.

Furthermore, applicants must provide three letters of recommendation rather than two, with at least one letter written by an industry supervisor or internship mentor.

### Deadline Warning
The deadline on the applicant dashboard is set for November 30, 2026. However, recommendation letters must be uploaded by professors 48 hours prior to the final cutoff.
