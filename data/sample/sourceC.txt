# Global Horizon Foundation Archives & Previous Grant Guidelines
**Archived Announcement — 2023-2024 Award Cycle**

### Global Horizon STEM Excellence Scholarship Notice
This archived page summarizes the terms for recipients of the 2023-2024 academic cycle.

### Key Deadlines & Cycle Notes
- Application Submission Deadline: December 15, 2023. Late submissions were archived without evaluation.
- Award Notification Date: February 28, 2024.

### Previous Criteria & Awards
- Scholarship amount was $10,000 per academic year distributed across two semesters.
- Minimum cumulative GPA required was 3.50.
- Renewal terms for the 2023 cohort required maintaining a minimum 3.4 GPA and 15 hours of community STEM outreach each semester.

### Important Notice
Please note that these rules and dates pertain exclusively to the closed 2023-2024 cycle. Current students should navigate to the updated portal for active deadlines and criteria.
